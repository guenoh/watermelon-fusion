FamilySansKR-Semibold.ttf is a static weight-650 Korean/Latin subset of Noto Sans
KR, renamed Family Sans KR. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanskr
The original copyright and SIL Open Font License are retained in OFL.txt and the
font's name table. The subset retains Hangul, Jamo, Latin, and basic punctuation.
