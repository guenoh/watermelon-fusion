# Personal portrait artwork

`face-atlas.png` is personal family artwork prepared from photos supplied by the
owner of this fork. The owner approved its publication for this family game.
It is separate from the original Watermelon Fusion assets and is not offered
under the original game's CC BY-NC-SA license. No permission is granted here for
third parties to reuse the personal portraits. Replace this artwork with your
own images if you reuse or redistribute this fork.

The atlas was prepared with OpenAI's built-in image editing tool to isolate the
five photographed heads. Original full photos are not included in this folder.
